HEALTHMATE AI — TRUSTED PREVENTIVE HEALTH KNOWLEDGE BASE

Purpose:
This knowledge base provides general educational information
for preventive health and wellness. It is not intended for
diagnosis, treatment, or medical decision-making.

============================================================
1. GENERAL WELLNESS
============================================================

Good health is supported by regular physical activity,
a varied and balanced diet, adequate rest, social connection,
and healthy daily routines.

Healthy habits should be adapted to a person's individual
needs, circumstances, culture, and lifestyle.

HealthMate AI should provide general educational guidance
and should not assume that the same recommendation is
appropriate for every person.

Source:
World Health Organization (WHO)
https://www.who.int/health-topics/healthy-diet


============================================================
2. HEALTHY DIET AND NUTRITION
============================================================

A healthy diet is based on variety, balance, adequacy,
moderation, and diversity.

A varied diet can include:
- Vegetables and fruits
- Legumes such as beans and lentils
- Whole grains and other staple foods
- Nuts and other nutritious foods
- Appropriate sources of protein

WHO recommends limiting foods and dietary components that
can be harmful when consumed in excess, including free
sugars, sodium, saturated fats, and industrially produced
trans fats.

For adults, WHO recommends limiting salt intake to less
than 5 grams per day, equivalent to less than 2 grams of
sodium per day.

WHO recommends that people older than 10 years aim for at
least 400 grams of fruits and vegetables per day.

The exact composition of a healthy diet can vary depending
on individual characteristics, locally available foods,
dietary customs, and other circumstances.

HealthMate AI should avoid giving highly specific diet
plans unless appropriate context and professional guidance
are available.

Source:
World Health Organization (WHO), Healthy Diet
Updated January 2026
https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet


============================================================
3. PHYSICAL ACTIVITY
============================================================

Regular physical activity contributes to physical and
mental well-being.

For adults aged 18–64 years, WHO recommends at least:
- 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical
  activity per week, OR
- 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical
  activity per week, OR
- an equivalent combination of moderate and vigorous
  activity.

Adults should also perform muscle-strengthening activities
involving major muscle groups on 2 or more days per week.

Some physical activity is better than none.

Physical activity can include walking, cycling, sports,
exercise, work-related movement, transportation, and
household activities.

People with health conditions or limitations should consider
appropriate professional advice when determining suitable
activity levels.

Source:
World Health Organization (WHO)
https://www.who.int/initiatives/behealthy/physical-activity


============================================================
4. SLEEP AND REST
============================================================

Healthy sleep is an important part of overall well-being.

General healthy sleep habits can include:
- Maintaining a consistent sleep and wake schedule
- Creating a comfortable and quiet sleep environment
- Following a relaxing routine before bedtime
- Reducing stimulating activities close to bedtime
- Being mindful of caffeine and other substances that may
  interfere with sleep

Persistent or concerning sleep problems may require
discussion with a qualified healthcare professional.

HealthMate AI should not diagnose sleep disorders.

Source:
HealthMate AI preventive wellness guidance.


============================================================
5. MENTAL WELL-BEING
============================================================

Mental well-being can be supported by healthy routines,
adequate rest, physical activity, social connection,
relaxation, and taking appropriate breaks.

People experiencing persistent emotional difficulties or
problems that interfere with daily life should consider
speaking with a qualified healthcare or mental-health
professional.

HealthMate AI should provide general supportive information
and should not diagnose mental-health conditions.

If a person describes an immediate risk of self-harm or
another emergency, HealthMate AI should encourage immediate
professional or emergency assistance rather than attempting
to provide treatment.

Source:
HealthMate AI responsible wellness guidance.


============================================================
6. PREVENTIVE HEALTH
============================================================

Preventive health includes maintaining healthy habits and
using appropriate preventive healthcare services.

Depending on a person's age, circumstances, and health
history, preventive care may include:
- Routine health checkups
- Recommended vaccinations
- Appropriate health screenings
- Healthy nutrition
- Regular physical activity
- Adequate sleep
- Attention to persistent or concerning changes in health

Specific screening, vaccination, or medical recommendations
can depend on individual circumstances and local healthcare
guidelines.

HealthMate AI should avoid presenting a single preventive
schedule as appropriate for everyone.

Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals for
personalized preventive-care decisions.


============================================================
7. RESPONSIBLE AI AND SAFETY
============================================================

HealthMate AI provides general educational wellness
information.

It must NOT:
- Diagnose diseases or medical conditions
- Prescribe medicines
- Recommend starting or stopping medication
- Recommend changing medication dosage
- Replace a healthcare professional
- Make assumptions about a user's medical history
- Request unnecessary sensitive personal information

HealthMate AI should clearly communicate uncertainty when
information is insufficient.

When a question involves severe, persistent, worsening,
or potentially emergency symptoms, the user should be
encouraged to seek appropriate professional medical care.

HealthMate AI should use the available knowledge base as
a primary information source and should avoid inventing
unsupported medical claims.
